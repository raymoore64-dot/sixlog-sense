Privacy Policy

Sixlog Sense by Studio Linicro. Effective 21 November 2026

Summary

Sixlog Sense does not collect any data. Your study progress stays on your device. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not operate servers that receive your information.

Information processed on your device

Answers, known cards, spaced-repetition state, study-plan settings, streaks and whether you have unlocked the full app are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. This information is used to resume your progress and is not sent to Studio Linicro.

In-app purchase

Unlocking the full app is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles the payment, the receipt and Restore purchase. Studio Linicro does not receive your payment card details and does not run a billing server. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

Sixlog Sense asks for notification permission only if you set a daily study reminder in the study plan. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device; it is not sent through our servers. You can change notification access at any time in Settings. The app does not use the camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or tracking permission.

No tracking, analytics or advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not include a sign-in. Study content is bundled with the app so you can use it offline.

Device backups

We do not operate a cloud service for your progress. If you back up this device with iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the app's local data in that backup under your Apple ID. That backup is controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

Children

Sixlog Sense is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Educational use

Sixlog Sense is an independent study aid. It is not medical advice and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HSPA, IAHCSMM, CBSPD or AAMI.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
