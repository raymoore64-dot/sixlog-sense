Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Sixlog Sense has no sign-up, no login and no account.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your device. Your answers, flashcard progress, study plan and streak stay locally on the device. We do not run any servers that receive this information.

Does it work offline?
Yes. Lessons, flashcards, scenarios, the practice exam, Release or reject, and the quick reference are bundled in the app and work without an internet connection.

What does the one-time purchase unlock?
The app is free to start. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks the remaining questions, flashcards, Learn modules and the practice exam. There is no subscription. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple. A Restore purchase button is on the same unlock screen.

How do daily reminders work?
If you set a time in the study plan, the app can schedule a repeating local notification on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you set a reminder. Turning the reminder off cancels it. Reminder text never leaves your device.

No. It is an independent study aid for education and exam practice.
